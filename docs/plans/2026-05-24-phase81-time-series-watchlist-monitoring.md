# Phase 81: Time-series Signals into Watchlist Continuous Monitoring v1

## Objective
Load Phase 80 time-series signals, establish baselines, detect deltas, run threshold rules, identify anomalies, and integrate monitoring evidence into watchlist intelligence.

## Core Boundaries
- Monitoring signal != trade signal
- Anomaly != buy/sell recommendation
- Strengthened != confirmed
- Revenue trend != customer share
- Gross margin trend != product mix
- R&D trend != commercial success
- No mock/fixture/raw/OCR/browser
- No pending/order/trade
